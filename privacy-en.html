Privacy Policy

App: Jigsaw Fantasia
Provider: tsukinoha
Contact: tsukinoha.games@gmail.com
Last updated: 2026-10-16

1. Summary

This app stores your progress and settings only on your own device. The provider cannot read them.

The app does, however, show video ads. To show them, the ad provider (Google) collects some information about you and shares it with Google and its partners. What is collected is listed in section 4.

The app itself never sends your information to a server run by the provider (the provider runs no server).

2. What is stored on your device

The following is stored in the app's own storage area on your device. None of it is sent anywhere.

WhatWhy
Your progress (stages solved, pictures collected, attempts)So you can continue where you left off
In-game coinsTo run the game
Lives, and when the next one is restoredTo count life recovery
How many videos you watched today to restore a lifeTo count the daily limit
Music, sound effect and vibration settings, and the display languageTo remember your settings
Display name (a placeholder the app makes up)To show your own row in the records list

The display name is generated by the app (for example, Player123456). No other player ever sees it. The records list only ever contains your own records.

3. Device features the app uses

  • Notifications: used to tell you when a life has been restored. They are scheduled on the device itself and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app's settings or in your device settings.
  • Network: used to load ads.

The app does not use your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

4. About the ads

The app shows two kinds of ads.

1. Video ads, so you can restore a life or receive in-game coins. Watching one is always your choice. You can finish the game without watching any. 2. A full-screen ad, which may appear after you finish a stage. This one is not your choice.

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure them, AdMob collects the following and shares it with Google and its partners:

Collected and sharedUsed for
IP address (may be used to estimate an approximate location)Serving ads, measuring them, preventing fraud
In-app actions (such as watching or closing an ad)The same
Diagnostic information (operation logs, device model)The same
Device and account identifiers (such as the advertising ID)The same

About the IP address: Google may use it to estimate the approximate location of the device (roughly country or region level). Precise location, such as GPS, is not collected.

Google collects this, not the provider. The provider receives only aggregate delivery statistics, and never anything that identifies an individual user.

How Google handles this information is governed by Google's own policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. On Android this is under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact place varies by device). Deleting it stops ads being tailored to your interests; ads themselves will still appear.

5. Sharing with third parties

The provider does not share your information with anyone. As described in section 4, the ad provider collects and shares information; that does not pass through the provider.

The app contains no analytics and no crash reporting.

6. Deleting your data

Everything the app stores on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. There is no "delete my data" button inside the app.

Your advertising ID can be deleted or reset in your device settings, as described in section 4.

7. Age

This app is not aimed at any particular age group, and is not offered as an app for children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this document is updated and the date above is changed. If the app is changed so that more information leaves the device, this document will be rewritten before the changed app is distributed.
