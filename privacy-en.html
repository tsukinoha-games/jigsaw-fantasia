Privacy Policy

App: Jigsaw Fantasia
Provider: tsukinoha
Contact: tsukinoha.games@gmail.com
Last updated: 2026-09-17

1. Summary

This app does not send any of your information anywhere. Everything the app creates is stored only on your own device. Nobody, including the provider, can read it from outside. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

2. What is stored on your device

The following is stored in the app's own storage area on your device. None of it is sent anywhere.

WhatWhy
Your progress (stages solved, pictures collected, attempts)So you can continue where you left off
In-game coinsTo run the game
Lives, and when the next one is restoredTo count life recovery
Music, sound effect and vibration settings, and the display languageTo remember your settings
A display name (generated automatically)To show your own row in the record list

The display name is a placeholder the app generates (for example, Player123456). It is never visible to anyone else. The record list shows only your own record.

3. Device features

  • Notifications: the app uses your device's notifications to tell you when a life has been restored. The schedule is kept on the device and is not sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off in the app's settings or in your device settings.

The app does not use location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or device identifiers.

4. Sharing with third parties

None. Nothing is sent off the device, so there is nothing to share. The app does not use advertising networks, analytics services or crash reporting services.

5. Deleting your data

Your data exists only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no "delete my data" button inside the app; nothing leaves the device and the data lives only there, so uninstalling is enough.

6. Intended audience

This app is not directed at any particular age group. In any case, the app sends no information off the device, so no information about a user of any age leaves it.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this document is updated and the "last updated" date is changed. If a future version of the app sends information off the device, this document will be rewritten before that version is distributed.
